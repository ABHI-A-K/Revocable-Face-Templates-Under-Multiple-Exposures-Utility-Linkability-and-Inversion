E04 intervals are conditional on fixed models, PCA, sampled pairs and validation thresholds.
Identity multiplicities are shared across all overlapping test splits and encoders. Impostor pairs use both identity endpoints.
Intervals are pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted; a paired interval excluding zero is not a familywise significance claim.
At empirical 0%/100%, a degenerate bootstrap interval is not a population guarantee.
Validation FAR targets below 1/N impostors permit zero false accepts and can yield identical thresholds. Observed test FAR can exceed the target.
Complete discrete threshold curves are descriptive. No threshold is selected from test outcomes.
Higher attack acceptance need not imply better reconstruction; read the cosine and recognition results together.
No official full-CFP, LFW, E02-linkage interval, device-runtime, energy, or camera-impersonation claim is made.
